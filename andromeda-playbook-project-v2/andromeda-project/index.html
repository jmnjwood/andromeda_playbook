The Problem

Meta's algorithm changed.
Most ad strategies didn't.

Meta's Andromeda retrieval system now reads the signals inside your creative to decide who sees it. Generic, vague, or too-similar ads get ignored — by the algorithm and your audience.

🎯

Old targeting is dead

Age, gender, and interest levers used to do the work. Now the message inside the ad decides who sees it. If your creative doesn't signal a specific person with a specific problem, Andromeda can't match it.

📋

Generic prompts produce generic ads

"Write me 10 Facebook ads" produces copy that looks like every other ad in the feed. No signal. No distinctiveness. No reason for the algorithm — or a human — to stop scrolling.

💸

Near-identical creative wastes budget

Andromeda de-duplicates ads that look too similar. Running five versions of the same concept doesn't multiply reach — it fragments your signal and drains your spend.

The System

Three stages. One conversation.
Finished ads.

The assistant runs the entire workflow conversationally — pausing for your approval at each stage. Your offer in. Ready-to-launch ads out.

1
Stage 1 · Research

Find the language your market actually uses

Tell the assistant your offer. It researches public discussions, reviews, and forums in your market — surfacing five distinct angles, five specific pain points, and the exact words real buyers use. It never invents a quote or fabricates a source. If it can't verify something, it tells you and shows you how to research it yourself.

2
Stage 2 · Concepts

Build ad concepts the algorithm can tell apart

Based on your daily budget, the assistant recommends how many concepts to build — then designs each one to be genuinely distinct in persona, angle, and format. ChatGPT version: generates your actual ad images right inside the conversation using GPT Image 2. Claude version: writes a production-ready image generation prompt for each concept, ready to paste into any AI image tool.

3
Stage 3 · Copy

Write copy that closes — not just hooks that tease

For each approved ad, the assistant writes five primary text variations — each with a hook that stops the scroll and a full sell section covering the pain, the solution, the price, and a soft CTA. Plus five headlines, three descriptions, and a recommended winning combination. Two ads at a time, then it pauses for your review.
